# Differences from vanilla

Press A facing adjacent water to start Surf directly. Walking into water does not trigger it.

Skips YES/NO confirmation and used-Surf text, then delegates to native Surf execution/animation. Keeps badge and party requirements, NPC/sign/item/script interaction priority, and native busy checks. Biking and non-A interactions are unchanged. Disabling restores the native confirmation; no stats or saves are edited.
