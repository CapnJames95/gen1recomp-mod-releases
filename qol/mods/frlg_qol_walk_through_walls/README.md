# Walk Through Walls

**Disabled by default.** In START → QOL → QOL SETTINGS, enable Walk Through Walls. Highlight it and press SELECT to change its controls. Defaults: hold keyboard **R** or controller **R / RB**. Release to restore normal collision; this is not a toggle.

Only your manually directed field steps bypass solid tiles, elevation barriers and objects. Map boundaries, water requirements, menus, battles, transitions and native scripts remain in effect. NPC movement and boulder collision are unchanged. Finish crossing onto clear ground before releasing.

Walk Through Walls temporarily yields to normal collision while Surfing, underwater or mounting/dismounting Surf. Use a normal shoreline to land; holding the hotkey works again once on foot.
